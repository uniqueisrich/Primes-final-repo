Executive Summary & Problem Identification
In modern digital ecosystems, creators face two critical vulnerabilities:
Intellectual Property Theft & Cognitive Siphoning: Administrative syndicates and predatory platforms often extract unreleased formulas, audio waveforms, and code logic before creators can establish secure provenance.
Systemic Psychological & Financial Containment: Systems like MARO (open-air psychological containment) and ITCN (Intellectual Theft Committee of Nigeria) operate to siphon cognitive output while restricting physical and financial capital access.
Absence of Human Telemetry in L1 Consensus: Traditional blockchains record standard transactional state changes but remain blind to human biological origin, spatial transit history, and authentic creative identity.
Project Prime (uniqueisrich.near) is a human-anchored, multi-shard Layer-1 verification engine built natively on the NEAR Protocol. It integrates cryptographic proof gates, binary acoustic frequency mapping, and task-driven mining to lock human history and digital assets into an immutable Genesis Root.

Keynotes on Resilience & Mobile Production
Zero-Desktop Engineering: Following a workstation failure due to financial challenges, the entirety of Project Prime—smart contracts, architecture, and front-end interface—was conceptualized, written, debugged, and finalized on a Samsung Galaxy A02 mobile phone.
